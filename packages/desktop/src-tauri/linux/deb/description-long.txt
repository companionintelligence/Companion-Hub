 Companion Hub is the desktop app for managing and accessing the
 Companion Intelligence Hub appliance.
 .
 Use it to install and manage apps, review Hub status, configure AI
 and network settings, and access your private local-first workspace.
 .
 Works without a graphical session: `companion-hub --detached` starts
 the Hub stack headless, and the bundled `cihub` CLI (installed to
 /usr/bin/cihub) manages the Hub entirely from the terminal — see
 `companion-hub --help` and `cihub --help`.
